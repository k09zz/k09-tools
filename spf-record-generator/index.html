FAQ

  
Where do I add the SPF record?
In your domain's DNS settings (at your registrar or DNS host), add a TXT record with host @ and the value above. A domain may have only one SPF record. If one already exists, replace it with the combined record.

  
Should I use -all or ~all?
Start with ~all while you confirm every sender is listed. Once your DMARC reports show all legitimate mail passing, you can switch to -all. Never use +all, which lets anyone send as you.

  
What is the 10 DNS lookup limit?
Receivers stop evaluating SPF after 10 DNS-querying terms (include, a, mx, exists, redirect), counted through every nested include. Going over makes SPF fail. See our guide: How to fix "SPF too many DNS lookups".

  
My provider isn't listed.
Look up "SPF" in your provider's help pages and paste the include (for example spf.provider.com) or IP ranges into box 2.

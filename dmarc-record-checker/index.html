FAQ

What is a DMARC record?

A TXT record at _dmarc.yourdomain.com that tells receivers what to do with mail that fails SPF and DKIM (nothing, spam folder or block) and where to send reports.

Is p=none enough?

It satisfies the Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rule, but it does not stop spoofing. Use it to collect reports, then move to quarantine and reject. See the safe rollout guide.

Why am I not getting DMARC reports?

Your record needs a rua=mailto:... address. If the address is on another domain, that domain must publish an authorization record, which DMARC reporting services do for you.

How do I create a DMARC record?

Use the free DMARC record generator, then add the result as a TXT record named _dmarc at your DNS provider.

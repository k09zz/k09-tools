FAQ

What is a DKIM selector?

The name your key is published under: selector._domainkey.yourdomain.com. Google Workspace uses google, Microsoft 365 uses selector1 and selector2, and many services use their own names.

How do I find my DKIM selector?

Send yourself an email, open the original (raw) message, and find the DKIM-Signature header. The s= value is the selector and d= is the signing domain.

What key size should I use?

2048-bit RSA. 1024-bit keys still work but are considered weak, and anything smaller is rejected by many receivers.

What does "revoked" mean?

The record exists but its p= value is empty. That is how a key is retired; mail signed with it will fail DKIM.

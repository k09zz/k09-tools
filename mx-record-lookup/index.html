FAQ

What is an MX record?

A DNS record that tells other mail servers where to deliver email for a domain. Each has a priority; lower numbers are tried first.

What does "null MX" mean?

An MX record pointing to ., which states that the domain accepts no email at all (RFC 7505).

Can I check whether email addresses are real?

MX records show whether a domain can receive mail. To check a list of addresses (syntax, dead domains, disposable and role accounts), use the free email validator.

FAQ

What is an SPF record?

A TXT record at the root of your domain, starting with v=spf1, that lists the servers allowed to send email for your domain. Receivers check it to spot spoofed mail.

What is the 10 DNS lookup limit?

SPF may trigger at most 10 DNS lookups (each include, a, mx, exists and redirect, counted through nested includes). Above 10, SPF returns a permanent error and fails for every message. See how to fix too many DNS lookups.

Should SPF end in ~all or -all?

Both are fine when you also have DMARC. -all (fail) is stricter; ~all (softfail) is the common, safe choice. Never use +all, which lets anyone send as you.

Can I have two SPF records?

No. Two v=spf1 records make SPF fail. Merge them into one record with all the includes.
